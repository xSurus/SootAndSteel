Game Title: Soot & Steel

Long Description:
Soot & Steel throws up to 4 players into a co-op fight aboard an iron train charging through a frozen wasteland. Stoke the coal engine, repair shattered walls, forge ammunition and man the cannon to defend yourself against Siberian thieves before the train is torn apart. Take advantage of automations and diverse bullet upgrades to stand a fighting chance. Every second demands sharp coordination, split-second decisions, and absolute trust in your team. Can your team survive the harsh conditions or will you be buried under snow, soot and steel?

Short Description:
A co-op survival game where your crew races to repair, reload and fight back against Siberian thieves aboard a roaring iron train.
